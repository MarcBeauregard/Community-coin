# Demo assets

These files are demo fixtures for the lobbies prototype. They are not part of the protocol and are never shown
outside demo mode.

- `coins/*.png`: logos of existing memecoins (dogwifhat, Popcat, cat in a dogs world, Brett, Floki, Dogecoin,
  Shiba Inu, Bonk), taken from the public token-logo collection `trustwallet/assets` on GitHub. The marks belong to
  their respective communities.
- `people/*.jpg`: AI-generated portraits of people who do not exist, made for this prototype.
